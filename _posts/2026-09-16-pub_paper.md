The study, previously available as a preprint, is now published following peer-review in Nature Communications!

In this study, as part of a collaboration with [Shimon Bershtein's lab](https://lifewp.bgu.ac.il/wp/shimonb/), we show that adaptive evolution can reshape the structure of metabolic networks, creating complex phenotypes that link mutations to fitness. Using a metabolically impaired E. coli strain, the team demonstrates that fitness gains arise from large-scale rewiring of metabolite correlation networks, producing smaller, more cohesive, and better-connected clusters. These results highlight metabolite correlation networks as a powerful framework for uncovering emergent phenotypes that drive metabolic adaptation.

Read the full paper here: [Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77850-0)
