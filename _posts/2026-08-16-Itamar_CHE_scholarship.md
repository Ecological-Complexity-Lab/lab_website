We are proud to share that Itamar Talpaz has been awarded a scholarship from the (israeli) Council for Higher Education! 
The scholarship will support his research on "Prediction of infectious disease structure in multiscale ecological systems", a project that combines network science and machine learning to understand how disease risk is structured across biological scales, from host traits to ecological communities. 
Congratulations, Itamar!
