On May 20th, we finished our very first Labathon - a lab hackathon! 
Two weeks ago the whole lab came together for a day of tackling a problem, planning how to solve it, and coding a tool that might benefit us all once developed.
Starting from the structure of it, how it works and finishing with building a prototype. 
Today we all came togather to hear the sales pitch from each of the colleborating groups (originally decided by random) explaining why we should invest in them specifically :).
It was a fun, intetesting and energizing time that brought out the best of the lab's collective creativity!
