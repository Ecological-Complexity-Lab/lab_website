Shai took part in the [ANdiNA VIII workshop](https://andinaspain2026.com/), held in Nerín, in the Aragonese Pyrenees, Spain. ANdiNA is an international "walk-shop" that brings together theoretical and applied ecologists to think critically about ecological predictability. It was a wonderful opportunity to connect with colleagues, exchange ideas, and reflect on the big questions in ecology, all while walking through stunning mountain landscapes.
