We are excited to share MiRA: a new interactive visualization tool for biological multilayer networks! Developed by Shir Nehoray and Yuval Bloch, MiRA makes it easy to explore and interact with complex, multilayered biological network data. The tool is designed to be accessible and intuitive, lowering the barrier for researchers to visualize the rich structure of multilayer ecological systems.

Try it out at [mira.ecomplab.com](https://mira.ecomplab.com/) and read the full preprint here: [arXiv](https://doi.org/hb5fwz)
