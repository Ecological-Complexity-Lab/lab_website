Congratulations to Itamar Talpaz for receiving an excellence award for PhD candidates from The Goldman Sonnenfeldt School of Sustainability and Climate Change!
Itamar's research on host-pathogen interactions in Madagascar, that uses network analysis and machine learning to understand the factors that predict infection risk across a human land-use gradient, is making an impact. This recognition is very well deserved. Good job, Itamar!
