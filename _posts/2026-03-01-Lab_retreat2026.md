In the last weekend of Fabruary, the whole group took some time away from the lab for a fun trip to the Eilat!
I was a great change to break the routine, to enjoy each other's company, recharge, and enjoy good food. We went to a wild life reserve, a bird watch center and even had dinner in a great restaurant!
Even if it was shorter then we would have wanted, it was a great fun, with a variaty of shared memories. 
Looking forward to many more!
